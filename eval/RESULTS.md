# Test results (synthetic test set)

Generated by `python -m eval.replay` from Prisma v2.5 streaming transcripts of the synthetic readings in `eval/testset` (Timbre voices Riyaa and Devansh; no human or child audio). The tutor is strict and line-by-line: a scripted reader cannot do word practice, so each reading is scored up to Buddy's first correction, and a false correction is a correctly read word that Buddy flagged for practice.

## Stress tests (noise and mid-line hesitations)

Built from the cached clean readings with `eval/make_hardset.py`: ~10 dB SNR background noise, and every line cut at its quietest point with a 1.0-1.8 s pause inserted. The cut sometimes lands inside a word, which is harsher than a real child's pause. `hard` (voice Devansh) was used for one round of robustness fixes; `hard2` (voice Riyaa, new noise and pause points) was not used for tuning. Tracking here is pessimistic: after any correction the scripted reader does not re-read, as a child would, so the pointer stays behind.
