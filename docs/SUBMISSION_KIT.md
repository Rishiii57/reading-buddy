# Submission kit

## 60-second demo video script

Open with the problem, not the tech stack (PID rule). Record the screen with system audio on. Use the **Demo reader**, or better, a team member reading live.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | A child's reading book, then the app's home screen | "Every child learning to read needs someone beside them who notices when they're stuck. Most don't have that person, especially in their own language." |
| 8–15 s | Pick ગુજરાતી → વરસાદ | "This is a voice reading tutor. It listens in Gujarati, Hindi and English." |
| 15–30 s | Buddy greets, reads the first line; the reader reads along (line turns green), then stops mid-line → "Listen…" and the next words glow | "Buddy starts like a teacher: it reads first, then it's your turn. Stuck? It gives the next few words." |
| 30–42 s | Reader says a wrong word → "Let's practise this word together" + the word alone; the child repeats it; Buddy reads the whole line; the child reads it again | "Every mistake is practised word by word, then the whole line, until it's right. It never says 'wrong'." |
| 42–50 s | Switch to English or Hindi, a few seconds of reading | "Same engine, three scripts." |
| 50–60 s | Summary card: words read, helped, words to practise | "Parents get a summary with the words to practise. Built on Gnani Prisma for listening and Gnani Timbre for speaking." |

## Post for LinkedIn / X (must be live before submitting, then nominated on the form)

> Every child learning to read needs someone beside them who notices when they get stuck.
>
> For the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge we built a **voice reading tutor** that listens to a child read a poem aloud in **ગુજરાતી, हिन्दी or English** and helps like a patient tutor would:
> 🟢 follows along and highlights the child's place
> 🗣️ says the next few words when they're stuck
> 🔁 practises every wrong word one by one, then the whole line again, and never says "wrong"
> 📋 gives parents a list of words to practise
>
> Listening: Gnani **Prisma v2.5** streaming speech-to-text. Speaking: Gnani **Timbre v3.0** text-to-speech.
> On our synthetic test set it caught 23/24 wrong or skipped words and prompted stalls within ~2.4 s. Next up: testing with real young readers.
>
> #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge #GnaniAI
