    About Reading Buddy

    A reading tutor that listens.

    What Buddy does

    • Follows along: the next word is highlighted as the child reads each line aloud.
    • Checks every line: when a line is finished, Buddy picks out any word read wrong or skipped.
    • Practises word by word: Buddy says each tricky word and the child says it back, until it's right. Then Buddy reads the whole line the way the poem goes, and the child reads it again.
    • Reads it with you: if the child is stuck, Buddy reads the whole line, then it's the child's turn.
    • Knows when to move on: after more than 10 mistakes on one line, Buddy says "Let's leave this line" and reminds the child about it at the end.

    Built on Gnani AI

    • Gnani Prisma v2.5 streaming speech-to-text listens in Gujarati, English and Hindi.
    • Gnani Timbre v3.0 text-to-speech gives Buddy its voice.
    • Our own engine matches what was heard to the text and decides when to help.

    Privacy and limits

    • The browser asks before the microphone is used for the first time.
    • While a reading session runs, microphone audio is streamed to Gnani's Prisma speech-to-text service to turn it into text. Buddy's voice comes from Gnani's Timbre text-to-speech. This app does not save the audio or the transcript. (Testers can switch on saving for consenting team members only.)
    • The microphone is on only during a session, is muted while Buddy speaks, and stops as soon as the session ends.
    • Buddy never asks for a name, address, school or any personal details.
    • Buddy catches wrong, skipped and stalled words. It does not score pronunciation, and the speech model can sometimes hear the right word even when a different one was said.
