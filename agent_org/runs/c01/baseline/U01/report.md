# U01 · Dhruv, UKG (5.5 y) · gu-IN

*Journey:* read_whole_poem · *Outcome:* **abandoned_frustration**

*Text:* મારો પતંગ (Gujarati, Grade 1)

## In my words

> The words were too hard. I can't read those yet.
> Nobody told me what to do.
> Buddy talked too long.
> Buddy talked when I was thinking!
> I pressed the mic to talk and it stopped!
> It stopped and I didn't know how to make it go.

## What happened (observed)

- **NO_TEXT_FOR_MY_LEVEL** ×1 (observed): UKG child landed on a 'Grade 1' text; nothing in the library is marked for Nursery/LKG/UKG.
- **NO_SPOKEN_GUIDANCE_BEFORE_START** ×1 (observed): On the reading page nothing was said out loud; the only instructions were written in English, which this child cannot read.
- **LONG_TUTOR_TALK** ×1 (observed): Buddy talked for over 12 s in one go before the child could read.
- **INTERRUPTED_WHILE_THINKING** ×3 (observed): Buddy started talking 2.3 s into a planned 2.32 s thinking pause.
- **MIC_TAP_PAUSED_SESSION** ×1 (observed): Tapped the big microphone to talk; the session paused instead.
- **HARD_TO_RESUME** ×1 (observed): Took 10 s to work out how to carry on after a pause.
