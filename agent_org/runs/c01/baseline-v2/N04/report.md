# N04 · Saanvi, Nursery (3.5 y) · en-IN

*Journey:* read_whole_poem · *Outcome:* **abandoned_frustration**

*Text:* વરસાદ (Gujarati, Grade 1-2)

## In my words

> This isn't my language!
> The words were too hard. I can't read those yet.
> Nobody told me what to do.
> I don't know what Buddy said.
> I said it but Buddy didn't hear me!
> Buddy talked too long.
