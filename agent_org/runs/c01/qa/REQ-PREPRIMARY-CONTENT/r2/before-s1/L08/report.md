# L08 · Shaurya, LKG (4.5 y) · hi-IN

*Journey:* read_whole_poem · *Outcome:* **abandoned_attention**

*Text:* मीना का बगीचा (Hindi, Grade 3-4)

## In my words

> The words were too hard. I can't read those yet.
> Buddy talked too long.
> I said it but Buddy didn't hear me!
> Buddy talked when I was thinking!
> It stopped and I didn't know how to make it go.

## What happened (observed)

- **NO_TEXT_FOR_MY_LEVEL** ×1 (observed): LKG child landed on a 'Grade 3-4' text, made for children who already read.
- **LONG_TUTOR_TALK** ×1 (observed): Buddy talked for over 12 s in one go before the child could read.
- **SPEECH_IGNORED_WHILE_BUDDY_TALKS** ×1 (observed): 2 word(s) the child said were not heard because Buddy was talking or the session was paused.
- **INTERRUPTED_WHILE_THINKING** ×2 (observed): Buddy started talking 4.1 s into a planned 3.15 s thinking pause.
- **HARD_TO_RESUME** ×1 (observed): Took 10 s to work out how to carry on after a pause.
- **MISSED_ERROR** ×2 (observed): Read 'पीले' as 'पीलै' and it was marked as read correctly.
