*Text:* बारिश आई (Hindi, Grade 1-2)

## In my words

> The words were too hard. I can't read those yet.
> Buddy talked too long.
> Buddy talked when I was thinking!
> I said it but Buddy didn't hear me!

- **SPEECH_IGNORED_WHILE_BUDDY_TALKS** ×2 (observed): 2 word(s) the child said were not heard because Buddy was talking or the session was paused.
