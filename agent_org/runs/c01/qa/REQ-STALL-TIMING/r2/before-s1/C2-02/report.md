# C2-02 · Neel, Class 2 (7.5 y) · hi-IN

*Journey:* read_whole_poem · *Outcome:* **abandoned_frustration**

*Text:* चिड़िया का घोंसला (Hindi, Grade 2-3)

## In my words

> It didn't say what to do, it was only writing.
> Buddy talks a lot before I get to read.
> Buddy didn't hear me when I was talking.
> Buddy said I got a word wrong but I read it right.
> I pressed the microphone and it paused.
> Buddy made me say the same word again and again.
> I liked it when Buddy said well done.
> When I got stuck Buddy told me the words.

## What happened (observed)

- **NO_SPOKEN_GUIDANCE_BEFORE_START** ×1 (observed): On the reading page nothing was said out loud, and the written instructions (English) are not something this child can read.
- **LONG_TUTOR_TALK** ×1 (observed): Buddy talked for over 12 s in one go before the child could read.
- **SPEECH_IGNORED_WHILE_BUDDY_TALKS** ×2 (observed): 6 word(s) the child said were not heard because Buddy was talking or the session was paused.
- **FALSE_CORRECTION** ×1 (observed): Read 'छोटी' correctly but Buddy asked to practise it.
- **MIC_TAP_PAUSED_SESSION** ×2 (observed): Tapped the big microphone to talk; the session paused instead.
- **SAME_WORD_ASKED_AGAIN_AND_AGAIN** ×1 (observed): Asked to say 'एक' 3 times.
- **INTERRUPTED_WHILE_THINKING** ×1 (observed): Buddy started talking 2.0 s into a planned 2.32 s thinking pause.
