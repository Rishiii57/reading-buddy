## In my words

> There was a box for parents, so I just ticked it myself.
> Buddy talks a lot before I get to read.
> I was just thinking and Buddy jumped in.
> I pressed the microphone and it paused.
> Buddy didn't hear me when I was talking.
> Buddy said I got a word wrong but I read it right.
> I liked it when Buddy said well done.
> When I got stuck Buddy told me the words.

## What happened (observed)

- **CONSENT_SELF_APPROVED** ×1 (observed): The parent-consent box was ticked by the child; nothing stops a child from confirming they are the parent.
- **LONG_TUTOR_TALK** ×1 (observed): Buddy talked for over 12 s in one go before the child could read.
- **INTERRUPTED_WHILE_THINKING** ×1 (observed): Buddy started talking 2.1 s into a planned 1.65 s thinking pause.
- **MIC_TAP_PAUSED_SESSION** ×3 (observed): Tapped the big microphone to talk; the session paused instead.
- **SPEECH_IGNORED_WHILE_BUDDY_TALKS** ×3 (observed): 7 word(s) the child said were not heard because Buddy was talking or the session was paused.
- **FALSE_CORRECTION** ×1 (observed): Read 'ચકલીએ' correctly but Buddy asked to practise it.
- **SAME_WORD_ASKED_AGAIN_AND_AGAIN** ×2 (observed): Asked to say 'તે' 3 times.
- **MISSED_ERROR** ×1 (observed): Read 'ખવડાવ્યા' as 'ખવરાવ્યા' and it was marked as read correctly.
