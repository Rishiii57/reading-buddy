- **LONG_TUTOR_TALK** ×1 (observed): Buddy talked for over 12 s in one go before the child could read.
- **MIC_TAP_PAUSED_SESSION** ×2 (observed): Tapped the big microphone to talk; the session paused instead.
- **SPEECH_IGNORED_WHILE_BUDDY_TALKS** ×3 (observed): 4 word(s) the child said were not heard because Buddy was talking or the session was paused.
- **FALSE_CORRECTION_STT** ×2 (observed): Read 'મારા' correctly but Buddy asked to practise it (the speech model wrote it differently).
- **SAME_WORD_ASKED_AGAIN_AND_AGAIN** ×2 (observed): Asked to say 'વાર્તા' 3 times.
- **MISSED_ERROR** ×2 (observed): Read 'છે' as 'છૈ' and it was marked as read correctly.
