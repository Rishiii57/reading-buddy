# C3-03 · Nisha, Class 3 (8.5 y) · gu-IN

*Journey:* read_whole_poem · *Outcome:* **completed**

*Text:* મારી શાળા (Gujarati, Grade 3-4)

## In my words

> It didn't say what to do, it was only writing.
> Buddy talks a lot before I get to read.
> Buddy didn't hear me when I was talking.
> I read it right but Buddy heard something else.
> I pressed the microphone and it paused.
> I liked it when Buddy said well done.
> I finished the whole poem!
> When I got stuck Buddy told me the words.

## What happened (observed)

- **NO_SPOKEN_GUIDANCE_BEFORE_START** ×1 (observed): On the reading page nothing was said out loud, and the written instructions (English) are not something this child can read.
- **LONG_TUTOR_TALK** ×1 (observed): Buddy talked for over 12 s in one go before the child could read.
- **SPEECH_IGNORED_WHILE_BUDDY_TALKS** ×5 (observed): 3 word(s) the child said were not heard because Buddy was talking or the session was paused.
- **FALSE_CORRECTION_STT** ×1 (observed): Read 'મારી' correctly but Buddy asked to practise it (the speech model wrote it differently).
- **MIC_TAP_PAUSED_SESSION** ×1 (observed): Tapped the big microphone to talk; the session paused instead.
