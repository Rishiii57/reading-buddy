## What happened (observed)

- **NO_SPOKEN_GUIDANCE_BEFORE_START** ×1 (observed): On the reading page nothing was said out loud, and the written instructions (English) are not something this child can read.
- **CONSENT_BLOCKS_CHILD** ×2 (observed): A written grown-ups-only box appeared; the child could not read it and could not start.
- **LONG_TUTOR_TALK** ×1 (observed): Buddy talked for over 12 s in one go before the child could read.
- **INTERRUPTED_WHILE_THINKING** ×1 (observed): Buddy started talking 2.2 s into a planned 2.17 s thinking pause.
- **MISSED_ERROR** ×2 (observed): Read 'મારી' as 'માડી' and it was marked as read correctly.
